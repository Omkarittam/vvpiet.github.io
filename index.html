ABOUT US

About VVPIET

Vidya Vikas Pratishthan Institute of Engineering & Technology (VVPIET) is an engineering institute located in Solapur, Maharashtra.

The institute provides students with opportunities to develop technical knowledge, practical skills and professional abilities.

VVPIET focuses on engineering education, innovation, practical learning and overall development of students.
