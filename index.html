ABOUT THE DEPARTMENT

Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering

Department Introduction

The Department of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering focuses on developing strong technical knowledge and practical skills in electronics, communication and modern technologies.

Students get opportunities to learn about electronic circuits, digital systems, communication systems, embedded systems, microcontrollers and emerging technologies.
